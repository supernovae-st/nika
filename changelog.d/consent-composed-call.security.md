- **`NIKA-SEC-014` holds a confirm gate in front of a child workflow call.** The consent check only looked at egress tasks written in the workflow itself, so a gate that the trifecta credited in front of an `invoke: workflow:` call was never checked to stop the child's effect on a « no » answer. `nika check` and `nika run` now judge a call whose child (or a workflow below it) holds an egress-capable task, whatever content reaches it, as that task: every route from a confirm gate to it must close on a refusal, as for the inline form, and the finding names the call. A child the check cannot resolve counts as acting. `check --json` lists the child task under `composed_calls[].effect`.
