- **`mode: article` reads the server-rendered `<noscript>` fallback when the page itself is an empty shell.** With scripting enabled the HTML parser keeps a `<noscript>` subtree as raw text, so a JS-rendered page whose whole body ships in that fallback (the Discourse forum shape) extracted to nothing at all. The cascade now re-runs on that source when, and only when, all three stages starved on the served markup; a page that extracted cleanly is never overridden by its own "enable JavaScript" notice. Measured on the WCXB dev split (1,497 pages, the benchmark's own scorer): forum word-F1 0.487 to 0.628, overall 0.7799 to 0.7906, article and documentation unchanged.
