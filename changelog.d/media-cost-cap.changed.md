- **Capped runs bill common paid media at dated catalog prices, and refuse what the catalog cannot price, beats included.** A beat always runs under its mandatory `plafond:`, and a plain `nika run` in a project with `ceiling:` runs under that cap. Under a cap, `nika:image_generate` on `OpenAI` (`gpt-image-2`, the default, `gpt-image-2.5-sunburst`, `gpt-image-2.5-flare`) or Gemini (`gemini-3.1-flash-image`, the default, `gemini-3-pro-image`, `gemini-2.5-flash-image`, `gemini-3.1-flash-lite-image`, `gemini-nano-banana-2.1`) and `nika:tts_generate` on `OpenAI` `tts-1` or `tts-1-hd` or on `ElevenLabs` (its models mapped to the price page's families) now run, floored at launch and billed against the cap, where 0.123.2 ran every paid media call with no bound. A paid call the catalog cannot price is refused before any request (`nika arm` shows the beat UNREADY with NIKA-1709): `OpenAI`'s default voice `gpt-4o-mini-tts` (no per-call price is published and its speech response reports no usage), `gpt-image-1.5` and `gpt-image-1-mini` (shut down by `OpenAI` on 2026-12-01), and any model id the catalog does not list. A beat on the default `OpenAI` voice is therefore UNREADY: set `model: tts-1` or `tts-1-hd` (`OpenAI` shuts every speech model down on 2027-01-06), use `elevenlabs`, the keyless `provider: system` voice (it bills no provider and settles as unpriced local work), or `local` / `mock`. Only a run with no cap at all makes an unpriced call.
