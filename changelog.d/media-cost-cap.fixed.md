- **`--max-cost-usd` and the run settlement now see paid media spend.** `nika:image_generate` and `nika:tts_generate` billed their provider and reported no `cost_usd`, so a capped run never charged them and the settlement left them out (a run whose only spend was such a call settled `unmetered`). The engine now carries a hand-sourced media price table apart from the models.dev snapshot: each row names its provider, model, unit (per image by size class and quality, per million characters, or per million tokens of text input, image input, image output and text and thinking output), USD rate, official source page and the date it was read (2026-10-27). The builtins bill every priced call from it, at the token rates times the usage the provider reported, at the per-image price times the images delivered when an image response reports no usage, or at the per-character rate times the characters sent, and say how on the output and the manifest (`cost_basis`, with the rates' date in `cost_as_of`); xAI images keep the spend xAI reports (`provider_reported`). The run ledger debits that spend as it debits a model's, so a capped run trips its cap mid-run, a `for_each` fan-out with no launch floor included (calls already started finish and count), and an `agent:` loop's media tool calls are charged the same way and judged against what the loop's own spend leaves. Under a cap every paid media call is judged on its rendered arguments before its request: a provider, model, `quality:`, `size:`, `n:` or `text:` only the run decides refuses NIKA-1704 when the catalog cannot price the call or its floor exceeds what is left. A paid call the catalog cannot price runs only with no cap and is counted in `spend.unpriced_calls`, so the settlement reads `unpriced` or `partially_priced`; a child workflow's unpriced calls reach its parent's settlement. The run's prologue records the table (`media_pricing`, beside `pricing` and `admission_pricing`), and `nika trace verify` refuses the cost replay of a journal that pinned other media rates. `nika check` names each paid media call on its COST rung with its floor, the rate and unit that set it and the table's date (METERED for a call billed from its usage only, UNPRICED, or DECIDED AT RUN), including a composed child's and the paid media an agent's tools can reach, and lists `unpriced_media_cap`, `priced_image_over_cap` and `priced_speech_over_cap` in `failure_plan`.
