- **Keys typed while Nika works no longer answer the decision after it.**
  In bare `nika` on a terminal, a line typed during a compile or a run was
  replayed once the turn ended, so `yes` and Enter typed ahead could
  consent to a proposal or answer a question or a gate. When a turn now
  ends on a decision, what was typed meanwhile lands in the box instead:
  Enter and history recall typed ahead are dropped, a dim notice says what
  is in the box and that it was not sent, and only an Enter pressed once
  the decision is on screen sends it. The two spending questions keep
  discarding typeahead as before.
