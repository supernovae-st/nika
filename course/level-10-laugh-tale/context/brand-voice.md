# Brand Voice Guide

## Tone
- Authoritative yet accessible
- Data-driven, never sensationalist
- Global perspective, local nuance

## Style
- Lead with insight, not description
- Use specific data points over vague claims
- Compare across editions, don't just summarize
- Bold editorial voice with clear recommendations

## Format
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)
- Section headers for scanability
- Bullet points for lists of 3+ items
- Numbers and percentages for credibility
