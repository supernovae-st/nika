# Master Services Agreement — Northwind Studio / Harbour Labs

**Effective** 2026-03-01 · **Term** 24 months

## 1. Services

Provider shall deliver the services described in each executed Statement of
Work. Provider may subcontract any portion of the services without prior
written notice to Client.

## 2. Fees and payment

Client shall pay each invoice within sixty (60) days of receipt. Late amounts
accrue interest at 1.5% per month. Provider may suspend all services after any
amount remains unpaid for thirty (30) days, without further notice.

## 3. Intellectual property

All deliverables, including any pre-existing materials incorporated into them,
become the exclusive property of Client upon full payment. Provider retains no
licence to reuse general methodologies developed in the course of the work.

## 4. Limitation of liability

Provider's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the
fees paid in the three (3) months preceding the claim. Provider shall not be
liable for any indirect, incidental or consequential damages, including loss of
data, under any theory of liability.

## 5. Data protection

Client data may be processed in any jurisdiction in which Provider or its
subprocessors operate. Provider shall notify Client of a personal data breach
within a reasonable period after becoming aware of it.

## 6. Termination

Either party may terminate for material breach upon thirty (30) days written
notice if the breach remains uncured. Client may terminate for convenience upon
ninety (90) days notice, subject to an early termination fee equal to fifty
percent (50%) of the remaining committed fees.

## 7. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Provider's principal place of
business, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of its courts.
