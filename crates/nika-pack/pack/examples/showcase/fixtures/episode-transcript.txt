[02:03] GUEST: A script either runs or it doesn't, and you find out by running

answer statically. What does this touch. What can it reach. What's the ceiling

change in ninety seconds instead of staging it.

[04:12] HOST: And if you can't?

[04:15] GUEST: Then your review is theatre. You approve it because the person

[07:38] GUEST: We had a job that read a folder of CSVs. The permission said

[10:07] GUEST: The lesson is that a permission is a claim, and claims need
