# Nika 0.123 — Product Convergence

Status: accepted product direction for 0.123, amended 6 October 2026 for cooperative intent resolution; implementation and qualification remain incomplete. This contract describes the target, not the behavior of released 0.122.

## Product and compiler

The user describes useful work, reviews an inspectable ordinary `.nika`, sees what was checked, rehearsed, observed and remains unknown, Saves without running, then separately authorizes a freshly admitted Run and receives Result + Proof. The normal production doctrine is **MODEL → SEMANTICS; NIKA → SOURCE**.

One compiler maintains the original intent, requirements, observed context, candidate and evidence. The LLM proposes the next useful action; Rust validates its preconditions, identity and effects and dispatches it through existing owners. Available actions include interpretation, Jev knowledge search and qualification, composition through the existing private Plan/Sketch, Check, supported confined Rehearsal, targeted repair and an indispensable question. Their observations update the same state until Preview, Save and authorized Run can produce Result + Proof. This is a cooperative loop, not a mandatory sequence of separate model calls.

IntentContract is the preserved satisfaction contract: goal, required work, inputs/outputs and their bindings, constraints/business rules, requested and forbidden effects, human gates, requested authority, postconditions, unknowns and provenance. SemanticPlan is a private proposed solution: operations, dependencies, bindings/provenance, capabilities, patterns, effects, gates, outputs, assumptions and unresolved decisions. Reuse existing Rust Plan/Sketch structures where sound; these conceptual names do not authorize a public DSL, file, SDK IR, compiler rewrite or renaming campaign.

Rust owns deterministic facts, types, graph/capability/policy constraints, hard feasibility, budgets, lowering, assembly, candidate identity, Check, consent binding, admission and runtime effect enforcement. Model output cannot grant authority or own final source bytes. A temporary direct-source recovery may produce or repair `.nika` inside the same resolution when structured composition cannot settle the task. Preserve its route, original obligations and the same checks; a successful recovery does not qualify structured composition by itself. Changing only the default to a less capable pilot does not satisfy convergence; creation, conversational revision, repair, CLI, Session, Serve and SDK must agree.

## Open-source sovereignty and SDK delivery

Nika is open source. Sovereignty means freedom to choose and replace the LLM, decision model, provider and access mode throughout intent-to-workflow, `.nika` and Run. The open-source user or deployment operator controls that configuration. Selected intelligences receive relevant work context without an additional sharing gate justified by sovereignty.

The current delivery scope is the engine and SDK, with reliable, measured behavior, documented contracts and real-use qualification. SaaS interface design belongs to the consuming product team. Expose available capabilities, intelligence configuration, progress state/events, results and diagnostics through the SDK; its consumer decides what to display, hide or configure. The engine imposes no model-display or model-hiding rule on a consuming UI. The TUI remains part of this delivery and qualification scope.

## Resolution and knowledge

For new free-language requests and contextual corrections, the normal Session path uses the LLM to understand the request with its relevant dialogue, active object, obligations and observed capabilities. Understanding and proposing a first action may share one call. Explicit commands, typed inputs and exact proven resolutions retain their direct paths without a ceremonial model call. A keyword, punctuation mark or template match alone does not establish complete understanding.

New natural-language creation and revision use one cooperative loop: the selected LLM interprets and composes, the selected decision model searches and qualifies Foundry, and Rust owns deterministic operations and controls. Jev is the reference backend discussed below, not a mandatory vendor. Model and access choices made by the open-source user or deployment operator take precedence over any local-first or remote-first preference. HOT/WARM/COLD/EXPLORE are historical routing labels, not target product modes. Reuse their useful operations and preserve old receipt decoding without imposing their old ladder. Exact commands, structured inputs and receipt replay retain direct paths. A service failure is visible; preserve work and identify degraded continuation rather than claiming the complete loop ran. Do not require ceremonial calls per node.

Foundry supplies admitted, versioned compiler knowledge by default where relevant: reusable patterns/blocks, capabilities/procedures, examples, counterexamples, repairs, known failures, relations, provenance and maturity. Retrieval supplies candidates, not truth, correctness or permission. The compiler revalidates feasibility. Measure FULL versus NO FOUNDRY with equal cases, model, DecisionSeat, budget and oracle; preserve harmful retrieval and zero/negative lift.

Foundry owns reusable admitted knowledge; Session/catalogues/host own current project observations; existing events and DecisionRecords link choices to their context and downstream results. This separates responsibility without requiring three stores. Reuse existing knowledge admission, graph, Snapshot and receipts. BM25 may supply exact-name matches, additional candidates and an experimental baseline; it is not the sole semantic filter that discards candidates before Jev can consider them. Cover the request's several obligations with applicable pieces from different families; retain alternatives, exclusions and NONE rather than forcing a winner. Qualify the selected set for compatible interfaces, versions, prerequisites, dependencies and effects. Load detailed knowledge progressively; a richer library does not mean injecting every skill. Distinguish authoring skills from skills consumed by a runtime agent. A semantically useful component that is not configured may remain an explicit draft dependency; design eligibility does not certify current execution feasibility or admission. Only a reproduced missing contract justifies a new field/index/relation, with producer, closed admission profile, consumer and fixtures aligned. No Palantir, DataHub, Backstage, OpenLineage, Graphiti or Dagster installation is implied.

DecisionSeat is vendor-neutral bounded semantic judgment over a finite known admissible universe, with distributions and NONE/UNKNOWN where supported. Jev is the selected backend for the cooperative search and decision roles; backend alternatives and Reflex require their own qualification. The current convergence program uses authorized API/ACP routes without local inference; this does not remove the product's local-provider capability. Hard filters precede selection; selected options are mechanically revalidated. No probability can override policy, resurrect impossible candidates or become consent. Group independent questions and elide a redundant sub-question only when valid evidence already settles it; record that evidence. This does not turn a lexical match into complete understanding or remove the cooperative roles from an open-intent journey. Candidate hierarchy/top-K/independent qualification must measure coverage@K and wrong pruning. No forced fallback winner after all candidates fail independent qualification.

## Intent coverage and contextual Jev judgments

The accepted target checks two different gaps: whether the requirement register
preserves the original request and active corrections, and whether the current
candidate satisfies those requirements. An incomplete interpretation cannot
become its own complete oracle. Keep source anchors, unresolved ambiguities and
conflicting requirements visible. Retrieve observations before asking the user
for information already available.

A Jev consultation carries the actual original clauses and active corrections,
relevant answers and requirements, the exact candidate or affected fragment with
its dependencies, observed inputs/capabilities, applicable admitted contracts,
and Check/rehearsal/run evidence with revision and scope. It asks a specific
question with explicit alternatives and an abstention path supported by the
selected primitive. A digest identifies this material but cannot replace its
content in the model context. Missing or truncated context cannot silently yield
READY. Group independent questions, preserve their bindings and distinguish
reported confidence from calibrated reliability.

Record what was actually sent, the selected backend and primitive, candidate and
context identities, answers/abstentions and observed usage. Do not request hidden
chain-of-thought or expose protected evaluation answers to the acting system.
Jev can identify a missing obligation, contradiction or plausible semantic defect;
its approval is scoped evidence, never proof of unobserved behavior or authority.

Semantic retrieval retains a path independent of lexical ranking. This does not
require sending the entire library to Jev: progressive retrieval, alternative
queries and measured candidate coverage must avoid making a lexical shortlist
the sole point of irreversible exclusion. NONE continues the same LLM loop with
explicitly absent knowledge rather than forcing a retrieved winner.

## Evidence, repair and authority

Rehearsal executes only supported copied, isolated or synthetic worlds. Protected real effects are never performed to obtain rehearsal evidence. Unsupported/NotRun/UNKNOWN remain visible. Discriminating worlds must reject plausible wrong work, not merely demonstrate that execution terminates.

The Behavioral Judge compares preserved intent obligations with observed behavior. Prefer exact assertions, typed comparisons, world/destination witnesses, then bounded semantic judgments, then an independent generative judge for open meaning. The proposing model cannot be the sole oracle. Evidence covers specific obligations, not unobserved requirements. A disagreement between semantic judgment and an exact witness triggers targeted additional validation; neither a confident vote nor a partial test supplies missing coverage. Preserve the READY binding law while migrating from the current whole-request judge. Mutation-test the oracle. Check green, successful execution, equality to reference source and a model's opinion are distinct observations.

Repair follows the same compiler, preserves original obligations, their source, policy, forbidden effects and observed usage, and retains candidates, observations and the best observed solution with its limits. Localize the defect, retrieve relevant repair knowledge, edit the affected fragment and recheck it and its affected dependents. Evidence is reusable only while its candidate, input, active requirements, capabilities and dependency bindings remain valid; invalidate affected dependents transitively. Compare incremental reuse with full revalidation before removing a whole-request control. A repeated failure changes the next strategy or observation, rather than repeating an identical request or stopping on an arbitrary creation counter. Deleting a requirement is not a repair. Wrong READY is a P0 and gets a forensic receipt, never averaged away or relabeled success.

Save and Run are separate actions. An already explicit authorization for the same action does not require another identical confirmation. Consent binds exact bytes/revision and cannot survive candidate or relevant admission-state changes. Fresh admission precedes real effects; a saved workflow, memory, Foundry record or earlier run supplies no renewed authority. Result reports usable outputs and uncertainty; Proof records observed evidence and its limits, not unobserved business truth.

## Atlas, Arena and learning

Atlas acquires permissible evidence of real automation demand with source/date/license/terms/provenance and semantic normalization. External workflows are witnesses of human needs, not unique implementation oracles. Arena owns independent contracts, discriminating worlds, mutations, protected semantic-seed splits, immutable attempts and accounting. Reuse existing implementations; no second benchmark platform.

Promotion: Atlas evidence and observed traces → consolidated reusable candidate knowledge → qualification outside the originating case and with relevant skill combinations → protected evaluation → measured downstream benefit → human review → Foundry promotion. A successful trace is not automatically a reusable rule; retain the source context, provenance, applicability limits and validation lineage of derived knowledge. Withdrawal invalidates dependent entries or triggers requalification. Validation used to select a rule is not an independent final evaluation; no online absorption of held-out answers. Protected identities and all paraphrases/translations of a seed remain on one split side. Previously exposed sets are development evidence, never quietly restored to held-out status.

Reflex/CLM are research/data/evaluation seams, not shipped product claims. DecisionRecords bind engine/spec/context/question/candidate-set digests, knowledge refs, backend/output/abstention, downstream behavior, oracle provenance, split and observable cost/latency. Keep collection scoped and secrets out. Prefer exact behavioral labels over teacher preference. Compare simple baselines/Jev/Kev through relevant real harnesses before expensive training; shadow-only until lift survives independent qualification.

CLM is identified as Contrastive-LM/CLM at studied pin bb42c6c5bf914fd449bed2f6ca65be80602cb1f7. Repository identification is not backend integration, role qualification, a current remote-main claim or a training mandate. Compare Jev, Kev and CLM by the actual role and preserve each adapter's option serialization, context limits and confidence semantics; no mandatory chain of three calls. Reflex remains shadow-only after useful baselines and must not delay Session/TUI delivery.

Project, conversation, persistent workflows, revision identity, visual task/dependency graph with drill-down, proposal, Check, rehearsal/UNKNOWN, authority/effects, Save, Run, live states/hierarchy, results/viewers, Proof and resume project canonical Session/runtime state. Do not create a TUI-only workflow model, permission model, fake run or second persistence owner. Inline/plain/accessibility remain usable explicit/fallback paths.

Session and compiler own the model → action → observation → continuation loop and progressive context; TUI renders their canonical state. Preserve candidate/revision differences, request obligations, resolved questions and evidence across turns, compaction and restart. A correction invalidates superseded work; a late result from another project or revision cannot replace current state. Cancellation reports effects already committed honestly. Record each model call, retry and repair with observed or unknown usage. Creation continues without arbitrary total call, repair, duration or spending caps; adapt to actual request capabilities and external availability, retain user Stop, and enforce the explicit workflow constraints for Run. Removing redundant global judgments is an immediate architectural improvement to measure, not a reason to weaken fidelity. The actual-binary journey also checks input while work proceeds, multiline paste, stable scrolling/focus and stale-result refusal. Present search, composition, checking, rehearsal and repair in understandable language. In the technical TUI, make the actual author model and decision-backend activity accessible; consuming SaaS applications choose their own presentation from the SDK state. ## Scope, change control and release

No V10, new public IR/language/compiler, SaaS/Control Plane/Connectome rewrite, template-count race or custom-model branding exercise. Before each new abstraction/field/API, the lane receipt answers the eight Socratic questions in the mandate: reproduced failure, reuse, smallest falsifiable implementation, kill criterion, independent oracle, responsible layer, ablation and user benefit.

One writer per shared contract. The integration owner controls architecture, integration and release claims. Each lane has pinned base, owned files, exclusions, dependencies, positive/negative acceptance and receipt. Final version sweep follows qualification, never program startup. Release publication follows repository authority. SHIPPED, MAIN, ROADMAP and RESEARCH/CONCEPT stay distinct on all active surfaces; historical evidence is preserved.

## Continuous creation and proportionate workflow constraints

Chat, clarification, search, composition, revision, repair, preview and restoration
have no artificial product quotas on attempts, tokens, elapsed time, conversation
size or number of requirements. Work continues according to remaining obligations
and observations, preserving the user's ability to stop or redirect it. A technical
request failure calls for diagnosis or adaptation, not an invented total-task cap.

Limits governing execution are expressed and reviewed with the ordinary `.nika`
and its explicit invocation. Size generated token, time, iteration and resource
parameters from the actual task, available provider capabilities, observed data and
expected output, with ample room for uncertainty. Do not choose a tiny default or
the cheapest route merely to minimize spending. Preserve user-specified ceilings;
when satisfying the intent conflicts with them, explain the conflict rather than
silently weakening the requirement or increasing an explicit authorization.

Storage, transport, parser and terminal capacities must not masquerade as task
quotas. Use streaming, pagination and appropriate context selection to accommodate
large work. Relevant original requirements must remain retrievable; silently
forgetting them or presenting truncated model output as complete is not adaptation.
Authentication, exact-byte execution admission and correct effect reporting remain
properties of the same product, not optional substitutes for quotas.

## Migration acceptance

Qualify retrieval, obligation coverage, evidence invalidation and native repair before
claiming the cooperative loop. Removing or renaming historical strategy branches is not
sufficient. Do not replace the whole-request verifier until the new evidence aggregation
preserves its coverage and rejects plausible wrong candidates. Keep semantic abstention,
unsupported rehearsal and provider failures explicit.

Current implementation, not qualified: the whole-request verdict remains the READY gate.
After a doubt (a rejection or an abstention), the judge's answers on each part of the request
alone, on why a part it finds missing is missing (the task it points to, or an operation no
task performs, never offered for a pure prohibition or a structure law) and on any extra
operation serve only to locate a repairable defect. A doubt with no located defect is put to
the same judge only over a rehearsal of the exact bytes that proves whole outputs, when the
sketch door has one and its host provides it (currently the Session): each part the bytes left
open, then, once nothing stays open, the whole request, carried only when the run's inputs
exercise every part; a part its inputs never exercise stays open, and a run never removes a
defect located in the bytes, at most contests it. The configured verifier receives those
trial inputs and outputs by default, as preparation context. With no such observation, or one
that decides nothing, the candidate is held: shown, never offered, its replayable record
dropped and its Applied `verify_held` finding worded by its cause (a rejection or an
abstention, and whether a judge call got no answer). Within a compile the same judge is never
asked again on bytes it declined; a host that keeps the conversation carries the judge's
rejections into its later compiles (`CompileRequest::with_declined`), where the same judge's
rejection of the same bytes for the same request stands with no call, while an abstention is
not carried and a new round may decide it. A plan replayed in an answer round, the reader's
own HOT plan aside, is READY only on a judgment of the replayed bytes made in that round. This
localization is evidence for repair, not the evidence aggregation required above, and it
replaces nothing of the whole-request verifier. The authoring provider's judge calls are
journaled under distinct roles and a decision seat's questions under their identifiers, so
their usage stays separable by role.

Compare business success, false READY, false refusal, autonomous repair, median and tail
latency, and usage by role on paired cases with comparable models, observations and oracles.
Retain failures and incomplete pairs. A faster selector is not evidence of a faster complete
journey; no performance factor is promised before measurement. API and ACP authors must
consume the same relevant knowledge and satisfy the same product contract.
