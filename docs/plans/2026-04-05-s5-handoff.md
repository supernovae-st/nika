### Architecture Insights (from agent)

1. The `cli_renderer.take()` dance (L1902, L2548) exists to avoid double-borrow of `self`.
   Moving the renderer to a separate struct would eliminate this hack.
2. Every early-return path (9 total) calls `write_trace()`. A `scopeguard::defer!` or
   Drop guard would make this structural instead of manually repeated.
3. The for_each aggregation (L2710–2858, 149 LOC) is logically part of the DAG loop,
   not finalize — it interleaves with artifact writes.

### Agent Findings — Corrected Understanding

The format_event gap agent found that **FetchExhausted, TaskRetry, ProviderAutoRetried
ARE handled in renderer.rs** (lines 1124-1160). They render directly with inline
