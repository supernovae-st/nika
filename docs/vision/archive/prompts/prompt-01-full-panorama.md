## Full Prompt (for detailed generation)

A sweeping bird's-eye panoramic battle scene inspired by One Piece's Marineford War — the open-source movement versus closed-source Big Tech. Eiichiro Oda manga style, watercolor texture, bold black ink, dramatic contrast.

### LEFT SIDE — THE PIRATES (warm golden sunrise)

An armada of pirate ships in V-formation sailing toward battle.

**Lead flagship — SuperNovae:** A ship whose hull is shaped like a planet with an orbital ring wrapping around it as a halo. Glows electric blue (#0000FF). Its Jolly Roger is a LARGE blue butterfly 🦋 with circuit-vein patterns in its wings on a black flag — the Nika symbol. A second flag shows a constellation of connected nodes (knowledge graph). The hull has five words carved in large text: INFER · EXEC · FETCH · INVOKE · AGENT. Blue butterflies swarm densest here.

**The fleet behind (each ship MUST have its logo LARGE and READABLE on its sails/hull):**
- Ship with a GIANT orange angular "M" covering the entire main sail — Mistral AI. Crew in French berets and marinière shirts. A French tricolor 🇫🇷 beside the Jolly Roger.
- Ship with a HUGE yellow smiling hugging-face emoji (🤗) as its figurehead, painted bright yellow-gold. The emoji must be very visible.

- Rust-orange ship with Ferris the crab 🦀 as a LARGE figurehead, pincers raised. "Memory Safe" on hull.
- Red ship with a T-Rex dinosaur figurehead — Mozilla. Firefox flame on the sail.
