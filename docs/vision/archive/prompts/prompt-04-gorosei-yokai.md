## Full Prompt (for detailed generation)

The terrifying reveal of the Five Elders from One Piece in their yokai beast forms, each representing a closed-source tech giant identifiable by PROMINENT logos and color schemes. Eiichiro Oda manga style, dark watercolor, horror atmosphere.

**SCALE:** Each monster is BUILDING-SIZED. They dwarf everything on the battlefield. Marine soldiers look like ants next to them.

---

**1. GYUKI (Ushi-Oni) — The Spider-Ox Demon [OpenAI]**

Eight massive armored spider legs radiating from a muscular ox body. Demonic horns curve upward. Fanged slavering mouth dripping black ichor. Dark black chitinous carapace with bristly brown fur. RED glowing compound eyes.

**LOGO:** A LARGE hexagonal geometric flower logo (the OpenAI symbol) glows sickly GREEN (#10A37F) on its forehead — unmistakable, prominent, filling the creature's brow. The green glow illuminates surrounding smoke.

**DETAIL:** Its spider silk web stretches outward across the battlefield, trapping developers in sticky API endpoint threads. The web is beautiful but a trap. Each strand is labeled with product names. The monster smiles while destroying — the most deceptively "friendly" of the five.

---

**2. FENGXI — The Four-Tusked Demonic Boar [Amazon/AWS]**

The LARGEST of the five in sheer mass. Four massive ivory tusks jutting from a demon boar's snout. Shaggy matted dark fur. Burning red eyes. Thick armored hide. It charges straight ahead, crushing everything.

**LOGO:** LARGE orange (#FF9900) arrow-smile logo (the A-to-Z Amazon smile) painted on both flanks like military insignia — clearly visible and prominent. The orange color covers patches of its hide.

**DETAIL:** Cardboard shipping boxes cascade from its back as it charges, tumbling and scattering. Its hooves crack the circuit-board ground. No subtlety — pure overwhelming industrial scale. "AWS" is scratched into one tusk.

---

**3. BAKOTSU — The Skeletal Centaur Horse [xAI / Elon Musk empire]**

**HYBRID CENTAUR FORM:** A human upper torso in dark elegant suit/kimono wielding a katana, fused to a SKELETAL HORSE lower body. Exposed white bones. Hollow eye sockets burning with blue-white spectral fire. Ghostly aura. The most visually striking — galloping at impossible speed, trampling pirates AND Marines alike.

**LOGO:** A bold white "X" logo burns on the skeletal horse's forehead. Blue-white (Twitter/X colors) flames trail behind.

**DETAIL:** Thin puppet STRINGS extend from his human fingers to CLEARLY VISIBLE silhouette logos dangling below — a bird silhouette (X/Twitter), a car silhouette (Tesla), a rocket (SpaceX), the letters "xAI". He controls them all. The Birdcage technique: a dome of strings descending over the battlefield, cutting free communication. Chaotic — he damages his own allies.

---

**4. ITSUMADE — The Surveillance Bird-Serpent [Palantir]**

Enormous bird-serpent hybrid. Sharp teeth in elongated beak. Serpentine tail coiling. Vibrant yet menacing feather coloration.

**EYES:** NOT organic eyes — SURVEILLANCE CAMERAS with glowing red recording indicator lights. Scanning everything.

**WINGS:** Made of military DRONES instead of feathers — each "feather" is a small surveillance drone.

**LOGO:** A LARGE geometric all-seeing eye crystal (the Palantir seeing-stone) GLOWS in the center of its chest — omniscient, always watching. The geometric crystal shape is clearly visible and prominent. Black-and-white austere color scheme.

**DETAIL:** The STILLEST of all five. While the others rage and charge, the Itsumade is MOTIONLESS, perched above, observing everything, reporting positions. Its stillness amid chaos is what makes it the most terrifying. It wears a white CP0 mask over its beak.

---

**5. SANDWORM — The Data-Devouring Worm [Google]**

A massive segmented worm erupting from underground. Its head: a CIRCULAR MAW with concentric rings of lamprey teeth — hundreds of inward-pointing teeth spiraling into a bottomless throat. It devours from below.

**LOGO:** FOUR-COLOR STRIPES run prominently along its entire segmented body like racing stripes — blue (#4285F4), red (#EA4335), yellow (#FBBC05), green (#34A853). The Google colors are UNMISTAKABLE and highly visible, one stripe per segment alternating. A constellation pattern (Gemini) glows faintly above its head.

**DETAIL:** It burrows underground, consuming all data beneath the surface — Google's crawling and indexing visualized. Its lamprey mouth = the search bar that swallows everything. When its head is cut, it simply regrows. Passive but all-consuming.

---

**IMU-SAMA — The Shadow on the Throne**

Behind and above the five monsters, at the highest point: a THRONE built from stacked gold-plated H100 GPU cards. Seated on it: a figure made of PURE LIVING SHADOW — not a dark-skinned person, not a person in shadow, but a being whose body IS darkness itself. No features visible except:

- **BAT-LIKE WINGS** deployed wide behind the throne, spreading like a canopy of darkness over the entire Marine side
- A **CROWN** with four impossibly tall, sharp, ornate spikes rising high
- **CRIMSON-RED ALMOND-SHAPED EYES** with thin ring pupils — the ONLY color in the shadow. Piercing, otherworldly, burning.
- A long **TRIDENT** in one shadow hand — a hexagonal geometric seal (OpenAI logo) glows on the trident head
- The shadow extends across the ENTIRE battlefield floor like an oil slick of darkness

An inscription on the throne: "No one shall sit upon this throne" — CROSSED OUT.

---

**ATMOSPHERE:** Dark storm sky. Red and violet lightning cracks across black clouds. Black smoke/miasma swirls during all transformations. Manga sound effects in massive text: ドドドドド (menacing rumble) and ゴゴゴゴゴ (oppressive tension). Cold blue server-light from datacenter towers in background. The ground cracks under the monsters' weight.
