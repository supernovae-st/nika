## GLOBAL CONCEPT

An epic panoramic war fresco mixing One Piece's Marineford War, Egghead Island, and Gorosei Revelation arcs — reimagined as the battle between Open Source AI (Pirates) and Closed Source Big Tech (World Government).

The battlefield is divided into three zones:
- **LEFT: The Open Source Pirates** — warm golden dawn light, blue butterflies, freedom, color, joy
- **CENTER: The Battlefield** — chaos, data streams, circuit-board ground, Vegapunk's broadcast
- **RIGHT: The Closed Source World Government** — dark storms, surveillance drones, cold server light, oppression

**Art Style:** Epic manga illustration in Eiichiro Oda's style — bold black ink outlines, watercolor textures, dramatic speed lines, manga sound effects (ドドドド), high contrast. Mix of black-and-white manga panel aesthetics with intense color bursts on key elements (logos, butterflies, flames, Gear 5 white glow). Textured paper feel. NOT clean digital art — raw, painterly, rough watercolor manga.

**Aspect Ratio:** Ultra-wide 21:9 panoramic (mural/Twitter banner format). Maximum resolution.

**Color Story:** Warm gold/orange/coral (pirates) vs. Cold blue/black/violet (marines). White (Gear 5 Nika) vs. Pure shadow black (Imu). Blue luminescent butterflies as the bridge element crossing both sides.
