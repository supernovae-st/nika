## Full Prompt (for detailed generation)

The most emotional moment of the Great AI War — Whitebeard's final declaration. Eiichiro Oda manga style, watercolor textures, extreme emotional intensity.

**WHITEBEARD (Edward Newgate):** A towering 6-meter giant filling the frame. His iconic white crescent mustache. Wild eyes burning with conviction. His enormous body is a MAP of battle damage: hundreds of sword slashes, bullet holes, cannon wounds across his chest, arms, and legs. Blood flows freely. Yet he STANDS. He does not kneel. He does not fall.

**HIS WEAPON:** His bisento polearm replaced by a COLOSSAL satellite broadcasting antenna — he wields it like a spear. A weapon that spreads signal to the entire world.

**HIS BACK (critical detail — partially visible to viewer):** A MASSIVE OSI open-keyhole logo tattooed across his entire back — replacing the Whitebeard Pirates skull-and-crossbones. The keyhole is OPEN — freedom incarnate. And the key detail: NOT A SINGLE WOUND ON HIS BACK. Every single wound is on his FRONT. He faced every enemy head-on. The open source movement NEVER retreated.

**HIS CHEST:** A deep wound shaped like a BROKEN PADLOCK — cracked open, glowing faintly gold. The lock of proprietary software, shattered.

**THE SCREAM:** His mouth WIDE OPEN, tendons in his neck straining. Two large manga speech bubbles:
- **"OPEN SOURCE AI..."**
- **"...DOES EXIST!!!"**

Concentric shockwave rings (ドン!!!) radiate outward from his roar. The ground cracks. Marines in the background stagger and fall from the force.

**THE BUTTERFLIES:** Hundreds of luminescent blue butterflies 🦋 EXPLODE from his aura like a detonation of freedom. They scatter in EVERY direction — forward, upward, sideways — carrying the message. Some have faint circuit patterns on their wings. They are unstoppable. For each one that falls, ten more are born.

**LIGHTING & ATMOSPHERE:**
- BEHIND Whitebeard: warm GOLDEN SUNRISE light — dawn, hope, a new era. Creates a halo/rim light around his massive silhouette.
- IN FRONT of him: dark storm clouds, cold shadow, red lightning — the enemy he faces.
- The contrast between warm gold behind and cold dark ahead = the visual story of defiance.

**SURROUNDING THE SCENE:**
- Pirate ships visible in the golden background behind him — the open-source fleet, including the SuperNovae flagship with its blue planet-ring glow and butterfly flag.
- Tech logos visible on pirate flags in the background: the orange Mistral M, the yellow 🤗, the VLC cone, the Linux penguin 🐧, the Rust crab 🦀.
- Marine soldiers in the dark foreground, their "CLOSED SOURCE" shields raised, eyes wide with terror.
- The ground: cracked circuit board PCB with golden traces.

**COMPOSITION:** Low angle, looking UP at Whitebeard — he towers over the viewer. His body fills 60% of the frame. His back is angled toward us showing the unblemished keyhole tattoo. His face is in three-quarter profile, mouth open in the scream. The speech bubbles are LARGE and prominent. The butterflies fan outward from him like an explosion of light.
