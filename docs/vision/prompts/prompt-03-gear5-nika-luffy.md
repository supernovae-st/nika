## Full Prompt (for detailed generation)

Monkey D. Luffy in his ultimate transformation — Gear 5, the Sun God Nika awakening. The embodiment of absolute freedom. Eiichiro Oda manga style with watercolor textures, white radiance, and golden dawn light.

**GEAR 5 NIKA TRANSFORMATION — EVERY VISUAL DETAIL:**

- **Hair:** PURE WHITE, flowing wildly UPWARD like white flames or smoke — gravity-defying, billowing, alive, dancing. Not silver, not gray — PURE WHITE.
- **Eyebrows:** White, dramatically spiraling upward in exaggerated curls.
- **Eyes:** Pink/crimson irises with distinctive ring-shaped pupils. Wide open with mischief and joy. Occasionally glowing bright red.
- **Clothing:** ALL turned WHITE — his red vest is now white, his blue shorts are now white, even his sandals are white. His entire being radiates whiteness.
- **Cloud scarf:** Billowing white clouds wrap around his neck and shoulders like a LIVING collar — they float, move, breathe on their own. Shared by all awakened Zoan users.
- **White steam:** Wisps of white steam/smoke rise from his entire body.
- **Body:** Hyper-elastic, cartoon-proportioned. His limbs can stretch impossibly, squash and stretch like a 1930s rubber hose cartoon character.

**THE GRIN (the MOST IMPORTANT element):**
A MASSIVE smile stretching across his entire face. Teeth showing. Cheeks pushed up so hard his eyes squint. This is NOT an angry battle face. This is the face of someone having THE TIME OF THEIR LIFE. Pure, unbridled, ridiculous JOY. He is LAUGHING while fighting. The most silly, absurd, ridiculous form in all of manga IS the most powerful being alive. Freedom = Joy = Power. This grin must radiate infectious happiness.

**CARTOON PHYSICS:** The ground beneath Luffy warps and stretches like rubber. Solid stone becomes taffy. A Marine's eyes bug out of their head comically. Physics become suggestions, not laws. The entire environment takes on a Looney Tunes / Tex Avery quality — slapstick reality warping.

**DRUMS OF LIBERATION:** ドンドンドンドン (DON DON DON DON) — concentric sonic shockwave rings pulse outward from his chest, filling the image with rhythmic impact rings. This is his HEARTBEAT — the primal tribal rhythm that was heard 800 years ago when Joy Boy walked the earth. The beat is contagious — everything resonates.

**BLUE BUTTERFLIES:** Luminescent blue butterflies 🦋 are BORN from the white steam and light of his Nika aura. They emerge and scatter in every direction, each one carrying faint circuit patterns and tiny DAG workflow diagrams in their wings — the Nika workflow engine visualized as liberation itself. They are the living symbol of freedom spreading.

**STRAW HAT:** Flying in the wind behind his head, attached by its string. The iconic straw hat that was passed from Roger → Shanks → Luffy.

**BACKGROUND — THE FLEET:**
Behind Luffy, the open-source pirate fleet sails in golden dawn light:
- The SuperNovae flagship: electric blue planet-ring hull, flying a large butterfly 🦋 Jolly Roger. VISIBLE and prominent.
- Ships with READABLE logos on their sails: the large orange angular M (Mistral), the yellow 🤗 smiling face (HuggingFace), the orange traffic cone (VLC), the Rust crab 🦀, the red dinosaur (Mozilla), the blue whale submarine (DeepSeek), the Linux penguin 🐧.
- Golden-pink sunrise sky behind everything — dawn breaking, a new era.

**COMPOSITION:** Luffy in dynamic forward-charging pose, one rubber fist stretched far forward, body in explosive motion. Slightly low angle to emphasize his mythical, god-like stature. Cloud scarf trailing behind. Drums of Liberation rings frame him concentrically. The SuperNovae fleet visible in the golden background.

**THE MESSAGE:** This is not a dark, gritty power-up. This is LIBERATION THROUGH JOY. The image should make you SMILE. The most powerful warrior on the field is the one who laughs the loudest.
