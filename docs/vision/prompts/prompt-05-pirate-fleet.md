The entire open-source pirate armada sailing in glorious V-formation toward the horizon and battle. Eiichiro Oda manga style, warm watercolor, golden sunrise, adventure and determination.

The most striking vessel. Its hull is shaped like a PLANET with an ORBITAL RING wrapping around it as a protective halo — matching the SuperNovae logo (a planet with a ring, containing a ship/astronaut silhouette with mountains). The whole vessel glows electric blue (#0000FF).

2. **Second flag:** A constellation of connected nodes forming a brain shape — the NovaNet knowledge graph.
3. **Third flag:** French tricolor 🇫🇷 — SuperNovae is a French project.

**Hull inscription (carved large in ancient-looking text):** The five verbs:
```
INFER · EXEC · FETCH · INVOKE · AGENT
```

**Butterflies:** Blue luminescent butterflies 🦋 swarm MOST DENSELY around this ship. It is the epicenter, the source of the butterfly wave that sweeps across the scene.
