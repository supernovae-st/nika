## Full Prompt (for detailed generation)

The legendary execution of Gol D. Roger — the moment that launched the Great Pirate Era — reimagined as the birth of the open-source AI movement. Eiichiro Oda manga style with warm sepia watercolor textures and historical weight.

**GOL D. ROGER ON THE PLATFORM (upper 60% of frame):**

Roger fills the upper portion of the image, seated or kneeling on the high execution scaffold. His features are iconic and must be faithful to Oda's design:
- **Wild dark hair** falling over his eyes — his eyes are HIDDEN, mysterious, legendary
- **Thick dark mustache** framing a wide mouth
- **Broad powerful shoulders and jawline** — a commanding physical presence
- **Red/maroon captain's coat** with gold epaulettes, draped over shoulders like a cape

**THE GRIN:** His mouth is stretched into THE ICONIC DEFIANT GRIN. Wide. Fearless. Almost laughing. He knows something the world doesn't. He is about to start the greatest treasure hunt in history with his final words. This grin must convey: "I've seen the treasure. It's real. And you'll never stop them from finding it."

**THE CROSSED GPUs (central visual hook):**

Behind Roger's head, where traditionally two Marine execution swords cross in an X pattern, there are instead **TWO CROSSED GPU GRAPHICS CARDS** arranged in the exact same X composition. The GPUs are:
- LARGE and clearly identifiable as graphics cards
- Visible fans, heatsink fins, PCB circuit board traces, memory chips, power connectors
- They catch the warm golden light, metallic surfaces glinting
- The circuit board traces glow faintly with green/blue light
- Rendered in the WATERCOLOR STYLE to match the manga aesthetic — not photorealistic

**CHEST SYMBOL:** A subtle OSI (Open Source Initiative) open-keyhole logo embroidered or tattooed on his chest — the belief he carries.

**THE STRAW HAT:** Resting on his head or beside him. On the brim, small handwritten text: "GPL v2" — the original open-source license, passed down through generations (Roger → Shanks → Luffy = Stallman → Torvalds → the next generation).

**THE ATMOSPHERE (behind Roger):**

Warm golden-sepia-amber smoky haze. Everything bathed in sunset/torchlight tones. The execution happens in a golden hour atmosphere of historical weight.

In the smoke and haze BEHIND Roger — subtle, atmospheric, NOT the focus:
- Ghostly outlines of **server rack silhouettes** rising like buildings in fog
- Faint **neural network** node-and-connection patterns floating like constellations
- Wisps of **binary code** (0s and 1s) drifting like embers or ash
- These elements whisper the future — tech hidden in the golden past

**THE CROWD BELOW (lower 40% of frame):**

A vast crowd of silhouettes looking UP at Roger from the plaza. Instead of swords, spears, and weapons, they hold:
- **Laptops** open and glowing
- **Tablets** and **keyboards**
- **Phones** held up recording the moment

Their faces show awe, fear, and dawning inspiration. Some weep. Some grin back, understanding his message. Among the crowd, visible pirate flags with tech logos: the orange Mistral M, the yellow 🤗, the Linux penguin 🐧, the Rust crab 🦀 — the future crews that will be inspired by this moment.

**BLUE BUTTERFLIES 🦋 (the seeds):**

The FIRST blue butterflies — just three or four — are beginning to appear in the golden smoke. Sparse, fragile, barely visible. This is the ORIGIN POINT. The seeds of the butterfly wave that will later become an unstoppable tidal force. Here they are newborn, tentative, but alive.

**TEXT ELEMENTS:**
- Faint terminal text floating in the haze: `$ echo "I left it all in that place"`
- Manga caption at the bottom of the image: **"Open Source AI... does exist!"**

**COMPOSITION:** Roger dominates the upper frame, the crossed GPUs form a strong geometric X behind his head. The crowd fills the lower frame, creating a vertical hierarchy. The golden haze creates atmospheric depth. The feeling: a pivotal moment frozen in amber — the instant before everything changes.
