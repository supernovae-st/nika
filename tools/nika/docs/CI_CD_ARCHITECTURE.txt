CI Pipeline:
  • Total Time: 35 minutes
  • Jobs: 11 (60 matrix jobs total)
  • Platforms: Ubuntu, macOS, Windows
  • Parallelization: Maximum (dependency-ordered)

Release Pipeline:
  • Total Time: 60 minutes
  • Build Targets: 6
  • Crate Publishing: Sequential with delays
  • Automation: Full (Docker, Homebrew, GitHub)

Coverage:
  • Threshold: 70%
  • Per-crate tracking
  • Codecov integration

  • cargo-deny
  • cargo-audit
