## 1. The Dark Enlightenment / NRx Movement

### What It Is
The Dark Enlightenment (also called Neo-Reactionary movement, abbreviated NRx) is an anti-democratic, anti-egalitarian, reactionary political philosophy that emerged from tech-adjacent blogs in the late 2000s. It rejects Enlightenment principles of reason, progress, and equality, viewing democracy as a failed system that should be replaced by centralized authoritarian governance.

### Key Figures

**Curtis Yarvin (pen name: Mencius Moldbug)**
- Software engineer, founder of Urbit (decentralized computing platform)
- Wrote the blog *Unqualified Reservations* (2007-2013), the foundational text of NRx
- Core argument: Democracy is inefficient and manipulable. The US should adopt a CEO-king model -- a "sovereign corporation" run by an absolute ruler
- Coined the term "The Cathedral" to describe what he sees as a conformist elite of academia, media, and institutions controlling society
- Key quotes:
  - "The difference between a monarch and a dictator is that the monarchical succession is defined by law and the dictatorial succession is defined by power"
  - "We'd almost all rather live under the 'autocratic' and 'absolutist' tsars than under Stalin"
  - "Democrats don't believe in democracy, they're free to hack it... when you hack it... it gets much, much more powerful"
  - His position is "always the opposite of Noam Chomsky's"
- Has called Peter Thiel "fully enlightened"
- **January 2025**: Guest of honor at President Trump's inauguration ball ("Coronation Ball")
- Peter Thiel invested in Yarvin's company Tlon
- Marc Andreessen described as "a friend of mine" by Yarvin

**Nick Land**
- British philosopher, co-founder of accelerationism
- Develops Yarvin's ideas with transhuman futurism in "hallucinatory texts of theory-fiction"
- Argues for the acceleration of capitalism and technology beyond human control

**Peter Thiel** (see Section 2 below for full profile)
- Described as Yarvin's most important connection
- Financed NRx since its beginnings
- Funded the Seasteading Institute (founded by Patri Friedman, Milton Friedman's grandson) for anarcho-capitalist utopias on maritime platforms

### Silicon Valley Connections
- **David Sacks** ("Thielverse" member): Appointed Trump Administration's AI and cryptocurrency "czar" in December 2024
- **J.D. Vance** (Vice President): Thiel mentee, known Yarvin follower, supported by $15M+ from Thiel's PAC for his 2022 Senate run
- **Elon Musk**: Echoes NRx ideas through DOGE (Department of Government Efficiency), focused on institutional dismantling
- **Garry Tan** (Y Combinator CEO), **Joe Lonsdale** (Palantir co-founder, 8VC), **Naval Ravikant**, **Winklevoss twins**: All identified as part of the NRx-sympathetic network
- Ideas circulate among tech elites without direct citation, influencing deregulation and anti-institutional policies

### The Board Coup (November 17, 2023)
- OpenAI's board fired Altman, citing he was "not consistently candid in his communications"
- Chief Scientist Ilya Sutskever led board opposition to Altman's "profit-driven strategy"

- OpenAI's exit agreements included clauses threatening to **revoke vested equity** from departed employees who criticized the company or refused to sign

## 9. AI Weapons and Autonomous Systems -- What Is Actually Deployed

### Israel: Lavender and Gospel AI Targeting in Gaza

**Gospel (Habsora)**
- AI platform that processes intelligence data (interceptions, imagery, electronic signals, databases) to recommend bombing targets
- Generates **100 targets per day** (vs. 50-100 in 300 days manually)
- 2021 test: Identified 200 of 1,500 struck targets
- Focuses on structures: command infrastructure, weapons depots, rocket launchers

**Lavender**
- AI database that scans data on Gaza's ~2.3 million residents
- Assigns each person a **1-100 score** for likelihood of being a Hamas or PIJ militant
- Flagged **37,000 Palestinians** as suspected militants (mostly in the war's first weeks)
- Sources report minimal human review -- often just confirming name, gender, and address
- Outputs treated "as if it were a human decision"
- **Collateral damage tolerance**: ~15-20 civilians per junior operative; ~100 for senior figures
- **+972 Magazine investigation** (April 2024): Six Israeli intelligence officers described Lavender's central role; "a mass assassination factory"
- **May 2025 classified database**: Only **17% of 53,000 Palestinians killed** were verified militants (per AOAV)

**"Where's Daddy?"**
- Companion system that tracks suspects and triggers bombing of their **homes** when they are present

**UN Response**: December 2, 2024 -- UN General Assembly passed resolution on lethal autonomous weapons systems (166-3 vote)

### Ukraine: AI Drones
- AI-enabled drone swarms deployed for autonomous targeting and path planning
- US military simulations (2018) validated AI swarms' superiority: 800 autonomous drones outperformed 1,000 manually controlled
- Eric Schmidt invested in D3 (Ukrainian military accelerator) and launched AI kamikaze drone startup (Jan 2025)

### Russia
- **Marker robot**: Serial production began March 2025; unmanned ground vehicle with AI for reconnaissance (100m vision depth), path planning, UAV coordination, and Kornet anti-tank missiles
- **Poseidon torpedo**: Successfully tested October 2025; autonomous nuclear-powered underwater vehicle

### Other Operational Systems
- **IAI Harop** (Israel): Fully autonomous loitering munition using onboard sensors for real-time radar target search, identification, and strike without prior intelligence
- **IAI Harpy** (Israel): "Out-of-the-loop" autonomous system that hunts radar emitters and executes attacks
- **OpenAI**: Signed contract with Pentagon (early 2026); forbids use in autonomous weapons only where "law, regulation, or Department policy requires human control" -- meaning it is permitted where no such requirement exists
